  1. Aug 2026

    Does Deviation from Prior Earnings-Call Language Predict Stock Returns? A TF-IDF Pilot Study on SEC EDGAR Transcripts

    Tested whether year-over-year change in a firm's own earnings-call language is associated with subsequent returns, using TF-IDF cosine similarity on verbatim Exhibit 99 transcripts scraped from SEC EDGAR — the first such test on fully public data. Built the end-to-end pipeline across 24 firms and 358 firm-quarters (2018–2024): the long-short deviation portfolio earned 1.27%/month (t = 0.88) against the Fama-French five factors plus momentum — economically large, statistically null. A power analysis showed the test could only detect effects of 6%/month or more, so the null is a detection-limit finding, not a rejection. Revised after peer review to narrow the claim, add a sentiment benchmark and expand to 30 verified references.

    • TF-IDF
    • SEC EDGAR
    • Fama-French factors
    • Power analysis
